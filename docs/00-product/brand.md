# FitG — Brand

Working name decided 2026-10-27. Status: trademark search and domain purchase pending (founder).

## What FitG is

The friend at the gym who tells you what to do next — and the friend in your kitchen who tells you what to cook and how much — so you never have to think about it. An AI agent that plans your week and pings you right before each moment. You confirm. You don't log.

**Positioning line:** Never wonder what to do at the gym.
**Second line:** You confirm. You don't log.
**The feeling we sell:** relief. "I didn't have to think today."

## The coach is FitG

- The coach speaks **as the app**. No mascot name, no orb, no avatar face. Its presence is a small pulsing coral dot before every coach line plus a warm tint on the "Next up" card.
- Personality in five rules (full guide: `docs/10-design/copy.md`):
  1. One sentence, then the button.
  2. Numbers from code, words from us.
  3. Friend, not dashboard.
  4. Funny at the edges, never at the user.
  5. Comebacks, not consequences.
- "Today's sorted." survives as a coach line (it's good copy), not as a brand.

## Look

**Warm Spotter hybrid** (tokens in `docs/10-design/tokens.md`):
- Planning and eating surfaces: cream `#FBF7F0` paper, coral `#F4683A` ink, round corners, Outfit + DM Sans.
- Gym mode and the lock screen: **always dark** (`#15110E`), oversized type and buttons, coral stays the action colour.
- Why both: the research says beginners are scared of the gym and overwhelmed by planning; warmth lowers the bar to walk in. At the gym, a dark room and a bright phone need contrast and size, not charm.

## Icon

Coral `#F4683A` squircle; a cream `#F6F0E8` mark that is a check whose tail becomes a right-pointing arrow ("done → next"). One stroke. Reads at 29 px. Dark/tinted variants: cream glyph on `#15110E`; monochrome for iOS tinted mode. Detail: `docs/10-design/store-assets.md`.

## What FitG is not

- Not a calorie camera. Photo logging exists only as a backup for off-plan meals.
- Not a chatbot. "Ask the coach" is a side door, never the main screen.
- Not a dashboard. One next step at a time.
- Not a drill sergeant. No guilt, no streak-shaming, no "cheat day" language (banned list in `copy.md`).

## Audience in one line

Gym beginners (0–6 months), 18–35, fat loss or muscle gain, worldwide in English with USD pricing; many eat Indian home food, which the product treats as first-class.
