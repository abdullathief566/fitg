# FitG icon, splash, store assets

## App icon
- Coral `#F4683A` squircle; a cream `#F6F0E8` mark: a check whose tail becomes a right-pointing arrow ("done → next"). One stroke, ~12% padding, must read at 29 px.
- Variants: iOS dark mode = cream glyph on `#15110E`; iOS tinted = monochrome glyph; Android adaptive icon = glyph on a coral background layer, safe zone respected; notification small icon = white glyph on transparent (Android).
- Export: 1024×1024 master (no alpha for iOS), Android adaptive foreground/background 432×432, notification icon 96×96.

## Splash
- Background `#FBF7F0` (dark: `#15110E`), centred 96 px mark, no wordmark.
- Held splash while fonts load; the mark's PulseDot breathes once.
